# Oburu Odinga Turns 83 as ODM Leader Says He Is Recovering Under Doctors’ Care

Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) leader and Siaya Senator Oburu Odinga marked his 83rd birthday on Thursday, 15 October 2026, amid continued public interest in his health and absence from recent public events.

In a statement reported by The Star, Oburu said he was responding well to treatment but had been advised by his doctors to observe strict bed rest and avoid public appearances as he recovered from an undisclosed medical condition.

## Birthday coincides with Raila Odinga anniversary

The birthday came on the same day as the first anniversary of the death of his younger brother, former Prime Minister Raila Odinga, who died on 15 October 2025.

Oburu did not attend the anniversary commemoration in Bondo, Siaya County. His message indicated that he remained aware of ODM's activities despite his absence from public engagements.

## Reassurance over his recovery

Reports published by Kenyans.co.ke on 15 October included photographs of Oburu celebrating his birthday with family members and Nairobi Hospital staff.

The developments followed weeks of speculation about his health and repeated statements from his family and party officials urging the public not to circulate unverified claims.

Oburu's reported statement said he was responding exceptionally well to treatment and reassured supporters that ODM's strategic operations remained active.

## What happens next?

Oburu's recovery and return to public engagements remain matters of public interest, particularly as ODM continues its political activities ahead of Kenya's 2027 General Election.

For now, his reported medical advice to rest means that claims about his condition should be treated cautiously and checked against verified statements from his family, doctors or official party representatives.
